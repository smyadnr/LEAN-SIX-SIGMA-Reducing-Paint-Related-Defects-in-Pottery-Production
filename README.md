# LEAN-SIX-SIGMA-Reducing-Paint-Related-Defects-in-Pottery-Production
My sister owns a small pottery business. Previously, she purchased pre-mixed pottery paint from suppliers, but the cost was high. To reduce costs, she learned how to create her own paint by mixing pigments and other materials according to recommended ratios
After using a new paint mixture, she fired 53 pottery pieces in her kiln. Of those 53 pieces, 48 developed paint related defects after firing, resulting in a defect rate of approximately 90.6%. The few pieces that did not develop defects either had no paint applied or only a very small amount of paint.
The high defect rate is creating significant waste, reducing product quality, and increasing production costs. This project will focus on identifying the factors in the paint mixture that contribute to defects and determining the optimal ratio of ingredients to reduce paint related defects and improve overall production yield.

GOAL: 
Reduce the paint related defect rate from 90.6% to 1% by end of July, 2026.


DATA:
I collected all 53 pottery pieces data.

TOOLS:
DMAIC 
- Define (5 Why, CTQ, Cost Benefit Analysis)
- Measure (Fishbone diagram -Root cause analysis, sigma level)
- Analyze ( Pareto Diagram, Process Mapping)
- Improve ( %s, POKA Yoke, SMED, FMEA )
- Control (Statistical Process Control)

RESULTS:
The project goal was to reduce the paint related defect rate to 1%. This target was selected because once an optimized paint formula is achieved, paint related defects should be minimized, with remaining defects mainly caused by painter related variation.  Although the target was not fully archived, the implemented improvements reduced the defect rate from 90.6% in the original process to 2.7% after applying the Formula B.
